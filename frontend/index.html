About HandyNaija

Bridging Trust Between Nigerian Homes & Verified Artisans

Finding reliable, skilled, and honest service providers in Nigeria has historically been difficult and stressful. HandyNaija was established to build Nigeria's most trusted artisan infrastructure.

Whether you are dealing with emergency plumbing leaks in Awka, installing solar inverters in Lagos, or renovating an office in Abuja, HandyNaija connects you with local vetted professionals in minutes.

Mandatory National ID & Proof of Trade:

Transparent Direct Communication:
Chat directly via in-app messaging, clarify pricing upfront, and track job progress in real-time.
✓
Hyper-Local Neighborhood Matching:
Find certified technicians within your exact district and area to cut travel delays.
